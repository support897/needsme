## Item 12 - AI vs AI workforce (simple words)
Sources: braininventory.in/posts/ai-agents-vs-ai-workforces, autonoms.ai, sanafai.com/blog/ai-workforce, rakita.io.
- One AI = one helper. You ask, it does one job.
- AI workforce = a team of AIs. Each has its own job, they pass work to each other, and they tell a person when they are stuck.
- For NeedsMe: a single AI (Claude, ChatGPT...) is one row. A workforce is the group of them seen together: who is busy, who needs her, what each finished.
- App wording to use: "Your AIs" (each one) and "Your workforce" (all together, what needs you). Not yet added to the app UI.
