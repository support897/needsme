Needs Me — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Needs Me is a personal to-do app for one account holder. It is operated by Ilse Placencia (hello@ilseplacencia.shop).

What the app holds

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • The content you put in: tasks, notes, dates, reminders and updates shown in your feed.
  • If you turn notifications on, a push token for your device so reminders can reach it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising. No ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Where it lives

Your content is stored in a private database (Supabase) and travels over encrypted connections. Only your signed-in account can read your list.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off any time in the app or your phone settings.
  • Ask for your account and all its content to be deleted: email hello@ilseplacencia.shop. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date at the top.
