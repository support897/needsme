Needs Me — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Needs Me (the app is called NeedsMe Ai) helps you keep track of projects and to-dos, and lets you connect the AI assistants you already use. It is run by Ilse Placencia. Contact: hello@needsme.xyz.

Signing in

You sign in with Google, with Apple, or with an email and a password you choose. Accounts run on Firebase Authentication (a Google service). Your password is held by Firebase in hashed form. We never see it. You can also use the app as a guest: the app opens a temporary anonymous session.

What we collect and why

  • Your name and email, from sign-up or from your Google or Apple account. Used to sign you in and to reset a password.
  • An account ID that Firebase gives your account. Every record is kept under it.
  • Your content: projects, to-dos, notes, prompts, group chat rooms and their messages (including AI replies), and your settings. Used only to run the app for you. Stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud), Sydney, Australia.
  • Alert settings and a device token, only if you turn alerts on. The token is how Apple's push service reaches your phone.
  • Connection links, only if you make one (see "Links to your AI tools" below).

We use no advertising, no ad networks, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. Nothing is tracked across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

AI services: what is shared and who gets it

Needs Me sends nothing to an AI company until you connect that AI and tap Allow in the box at the top of the Connect screen. You can turn sharing off again there at any time.

When you use an AI you connected with your own key, the app sends your message, up to the last 20 messages of that chat room, and, when you ask it to sort or check your work, the project and to-do names involved. This goes straight from your phone to the company you chose. It does not pass through our servers. That company's own terms and privacy policy then apply, including how long it keeps the data.

The companies you can choose are: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Perplexity, xAI (Grok), Meta (Muse), DeepSeek, Mistral AI, Groq, OpenRouter, and any other service with an OpenAI-style API that you add yourself in "Any other model". Needs Me only talks to the ones you set up.

Your AI keys are kept in this phone's Keychain, on this device only, and are not sent to us. Exception: if you tap "Bring my keys in", the app sends your sign-in token to our key service (keys.ilseplacencia.shop), which returns keys you earlier saved to your own account on the Needs Me web app.

Links to your AI tools (the bridge)

If you make a link so that Claude, ChatGPT, OpenCode, Codex or another tool can read your projects, a small service we run on Cloudflare Workers holds: a secret link token (stored as a one-way hash), the name you gave the link, a copy of your project names, open to-do names and counts, short messages waiting for an AI in a group chat, updates the AI sends back, the time each AI last asked for your projects, and your device token if alerts are on. The AI tool you connected can read the project copy through your link. Anyone who has your link can read it too, so keep the link private.

Links expire after 90 days. "Cut off all my links" turns every link off. "Erase everything on the link" deletes all of the above. Deleting your account erases it too.

Bug reports and feedback

When you tap Report a bug or Send feedback, we receive the text you write, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and your account email (or the email you type, or nothing if you choose anonymous for feedback). We use it only to fix problems and to reply to you within 24 hours. We email you a confirmation, and the message reaches our inbox, using the email service Resend. We keep reports for up to 12 months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours any time at hello@needsme.xyz.

Where it lives

Firebase (Google Cloud, Sydney, Australia) for your account and content. Cloudflare for the bridge. Resend for report emails. Apple for push alerts. All travel over encrypted connections. Database rules keep each account separate.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it. In the app, open Settings, then Privacy and your data, then Delete my account. This removes your account, your content and everything on the bridge, straight away. You can export your notes from the same screen. You can also ask by email at hello@needsme.xyz. Email requests are done within 30 days. To withdraw consent for AI sharing, turn it off on the Connect screen.

Children

The app is not for children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data by emailing hello@needsme.xyz. If you live in the EU, UK or Australia you also have the rights your local law gives you, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.
