Quick answers

  • What does the app do? It keeps your projects and to-dos in one place and shows what needs you. You can connect the AI tools you already use so they can read your projects and report back.
  • Do I need an AI account? No. The app works on its own. AI connections are optional.
  • How do I connect an AI? Open the Connect tab, tap Allow in the top box, then pick an AI. Each tile has numbered steps, a copy button for your link or key and a button that opens the AI's own page.
  • Where are my keys? In your phone's Keychain. They never go to our servers.
  • How do I stop sharing? Connect tab, "Turn sharing off". To remove links: "Cut off all my links". To erase what the link holds: "Erase everything on the link".
  • How do I delete my account? Settings, Privacy and your data, Delete my account. It removes your account, your content and everything on the link.
  • Alerts do not arrive. Check Settings, Notifications on your iPhone, and that alerts are on in the app.

Report a problem

Fastest: in the app, tap the bug icon at the top of Home (or More, then Report a bug). It takes one step, and we reply within 24 hours. The star-bubble icon next to it sends feedback, signed in or anonymous.
